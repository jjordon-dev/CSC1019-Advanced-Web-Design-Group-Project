Icedancer

Icedancer is the 3rd mixtape by Swedish artist Bladee.
Released in 2018, the project was produced by RipSquad and Lusi and it soon became a fan favourite.
Titled by many as Bladee's "Magnum Opus", Icedancer features ethereal meoldies blended with Bladee's "cold" and "lonely" lyricism
to help create a detatched coolness, just like a cold winter night.

Unknown Death

Unknown Death was released in 2013 by Swedish rapper Yung Lean when he was only 16 years old.
The album materialised Lean's style between 90s hip-hip and early 2000s internet culture.
The experimental album features talents such producer Whitearmor and rapper Bladee.
Soonthereafter, they went on to work closely within their founded GTBSBE group.

Tiger

Tiger was Thai-Swedish rapper Thaiboy Digital's first album in 2014.
After moving to Sweden at age 8, Thaiboy gained prominence in the Stockholm music scene after meeting
GTBSE, and working alongside Whitearmor, Bladee, Yung Lean and others.
Tiger helped define Thaiboy's role within the group with the release of a very energetic album.

E is the debut stuido album by British-Swedish artist Ecco2k. Ecco was the last member of GTBSE who had not yet published a solo album work. After its unexpected
release in late 2019, it gained massive traction from fans due to its "dreamlike" melodies.
